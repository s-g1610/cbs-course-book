Part of term: Full Term runs Jan 25 – Apr 30, A Term Jan 25 – Mar 5, B Term Mar 22 – Apr 30, Block Week Jan 19–23. Day letters follow the CBS convention (R = Thursday). "Professor change" means none of the Spring 2026 instructors is listed for Spring 2027. "Application only" sections are not allocated through Course Match.

How "Get it at" works: the lowest tier where at least 70% of students who ranked the course at that tier got a seat in Spring 2026. "Favorite, risky" means even Favorite cleared only 50–69% of the time; "Long shot" means under 50%. If a course has fewer sections this spring and was already full, it moves up one level; with at least 50% more sections it moves down one level, but never below Great. Courses that were less than 75% full are capped at Great, because their low percentages come from prerequisites or schedule clashes. The heatmap combines all sections of a course, so a popular time slot can fill while the course as a whole still shows open seats.
